# Changelog

User-visible changes, newest first.

## Unreleased

No change.

## 0.1.7 - 2026-10-24

- The message that shows after an action, with its undo, is a raised card in the colours of the screen. It is no longer a black box in light and a white box in dark.

## 0.1.5 - 2026-10-24

- The icon of Settings is a gear, as in the other apps of the family.

## 0.1.4 - 2026-10-24

- The app is now called **Compound**.
- The new look of the family of apps: the Geist font, a stone ground, square corners and green
  for the main action.
- From 600px the rail stands at the side of the hand.

## 0.1.3 - 2026-10-16

- A new app icon, drawn in the style that the family of apps shares.

## 0.1.2 - 2026-10-15

- Chrome now offers to install the app when another app of the same site is installed.

## 0.1.1 - 2026-10-15

- Thousands separators everywhere, also in the fields: `200.000` in the Spanish format, `200,000` in
  the British and US ones. Typed numbers follow the marks of the format.
- Settings → Number and date format: show money and dates in Spanish, British or US format,
  apart from the language of the texts. Automatic follows the region of the phone.
- Loan and mortgage calculator: monthly payment, total interest and a table by year.
- Term deposit calculator: interest at maturity, monthly or yearly, the withholding and the APY.
- Savings calculator: an initial amount and a monthly contribution, with a table by year.
- Simple interest calculator: solve the interest, the principal, the rate or the term.
- Each calculator keeps its last values, and can go back to its initial values.
