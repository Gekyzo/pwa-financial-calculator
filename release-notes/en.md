- In Spanish, the annual rate is now called "tipo anual", and the rate option of Simple interest is "Tipo", so it is not confused with "Intereses".

- In Simple interest, the highlight of the choice to calculate sits under the option you chose.
- In Simple interest, a paragraph under the choice to calculate says what each option calculates and what to enter.

- The title of the app is in the language of the app, for example in the window and the list of open apps.

## 0.1.8 - 2026-10-24

- `Settings` › `Help` › `What's new` lists what changes in each version. After an update, it opens by itself once.
